Lecture 16

Energy and global existence

We prove energy conservation at the regularity of the constructed solution. We then exclude finite-time termination for perturbations of either vacuum and of the kink. A focusing example shows why the sign of the potential matters.

Lecture 15 constructs the nonlinear field for a short time. Its continuation theorem reduces global existence to a bound on the perturbation’s energy-space norm. The conserved nonlinear energy supplies that bound on every finite time interval.

Show answer

Answer. No. The nonzero kinetic term is cancelled by the negative potential. Energy is no longer a sum of nonnegative terms, so it does not control ∥ut∥2\|u_t\|_2∥ut​∥2​ or the existence norm. The explicit solution blows up at time TTT.

Summary

For the one-dimensional ϕ4\phi^4ϕ4 field near a vacuum or the kink, spatial mollification proves energy conservation for the actual weak energy solution. The nonlinear commutator tends to zero on every compact time interval. Positive energy controls the field’s spatial derivative and velocity. Integrating the velocity controls the perturbation’s L2L^2L2 norm for finite time. These bounds exclude the finite-lifespan alternative and give global existence. A focusing potential can lose precisely the needed norm control.
