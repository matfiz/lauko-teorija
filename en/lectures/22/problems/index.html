  • Exercise 22.3 Parameters at a two-vortex collision #

    A moduli space here is the set of critical positive NNN-vortex solutions with the vacuum asymptotics, modulo smooth gauge transformations. Use the following stronger geometric result as a given: this space is a nonsingular complex manifold, and the coefficients of P(z)=∏j=1N(z−zj)P(z)=\prod_{j=1}^N(z-z_j)P(z)=j=1∏N​(z−zj​) are smooth complex coordinates, including at coincident zeros. For its zero-coordinate description see Alonso Izquierdo, Garcia Fuertes, Manton and Mateos Guilarte, JHEP 01 (2024) 020, §5, pp. 10–11, equation (5.1) and discussion.     1. Count the real moduli and the moduli after fixing the mean zero position. Explain why ordering the zeros overcounts configurations.

    Hint

    Short answer

    There are 2N2N2N real moduli and 2N−22N-22N−2 after centring. For two vortices, P(z)=(z−Z)2−Δ/4P(z)=(z-Z)^2-\Delta/4P(z)=(z−Z)2−Δ/4 and the collision set is Δ=0\Delta=0Δ=0. It has real codimension two in the four-dimensional moduli space.

    Worked solution

    The leading coefficient of PPP is fixed to one, leaving NNN independent complex coefficients. The given coordinate theorem makes their space real 2N2N2N-dimensional.
