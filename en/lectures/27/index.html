Lecture 27

Zero curvature and monodromy

What this lecture does

We prove the evolution law for periodic matrix transport with its basepoint term. We verify a matrix representation of sine-Gordon in physical coordinates and compute the transport of its vacuum.

The KdV Lax identity of Lecture 24 describes compatibility of linear operators. A related construction uses two first-order matrix equations. On a spatial circle their compatibility controls transport around one full period and gives conserved traces.

27.1 Compatibility of the two linear equations

27.4 The sine-Gordon representation in physical coordinates

A classical sine-Gordon field is a real C2C^2C2 function satisfying ϕtt−ϕxx+sin⁡ϕ=0.\begin{equation} \label{eq:27:SG} \phi_{tt}-\phi_{xx}+\sin\phi=0. \end{equation}ϕtt​−ϕxx​+sinϕ=0.(27.7) For the circle application, assume ϕ\phiϕ and its derivatives are periodic with period LperL_{\mathrm{per}}Lper​. The statements below are conditional on such a field; no Cauchy theorem for this nonlinear equation is asserted here.

27.6 Conservation does not prove complete integrability

The theorem gives conserved functions labelled by λ\lambdaλ and rrr. Different labels need not give independent functions of the field. For example, a family Fλ=eλHF_\lambda=e^\lambda HFλ​=eλH, for real λ\lambdaλ, contains only multiples of one Hamiltonian, regardless of how many parameters are used.

Conserved quantities also need not have zero mutual Poisson brackets. For the canonical pairs (q1,p1)(q_1,p_1)(q1​,p1​) and (q2,p2)(q_2,p_2)(q2​,p2​) with Hamiltonian H=p1H=p_1H=p1​, both q2q_2q2​ and p2p_2p2​ are conserved, while {q2,p2}=1\{q_2,p_2\}=1{q2​,p2​}=1. A family is in involution when all its mutual brackets vanish. No such bracket calculation for the monodromy traces has been proved here. Nor have we proved that these quantities supply enough independent data to determine the general field evolution. Conservation, independence, involution and completeness are separate claims. A finite-dimensional Liouville–Arnold theorem alone does not establish any corresponding assertion for this field theory.

Summary

For a C1C^1C1 periodic flat matrix pair, the normalised spatial transport has monodromy Tt=[V(t,0),T]T_t=[V(t,0),T]Tt​=[V(t,0),T]. Its trace powers are constant for every fixed spectral parameter. The converted sine-Gordon pair satisfies flatness exactly when ϕtt−ϕxx+sin⁡ϕ=0\phi_{tt}-\phi_{xx}+\sin\phi=0ϕtt​−ϕxx​+sinϕ=0. The vacuum gives an explicit diagonal monodromy. These conserved traces do not by themselves establish independence, involution or completeness.
