Lecture 18

Kink fluctuations

What this lecture does

We derive the kink’s charge and energy, identify the domain of its fluctuation operator, and prove nonnegativity and the exact kernel. We distinguish this linear result from nonlinear orbital stability.

The kink is a stationary field with finite energy. We compute its energy and the quadratic change produced by a perturbation. The resulting operator factors into an adjoint pair, which determines its sign and its zero mode. The zero mode records the freedom to translate the kink.

18.4 What the linear statement establishes

No exponentially growing normal mode of the form h(t,x)=eγtu(x)h(t,x)=e^{\gamma t}u(x)h(t,x)=eγtu(x), with γ>0\gamma>0γ>0 and nonzero u∈H2u\in H^2u∈H2, can solve the linearised equation. This is a statement about the linear equation. It is not a bound on every linearised displacement: the exact solution h(t)=tψ0h(t)=t\psi_0h(t)=tψ0​ has growing H1H^1H1 norm, although its quadratic energy 12(∥ht∥22+q[h])=1/2\tfrac12(\|h_t\|_2^2+\mathfrak q[h])=1/221​(∥ht​∥22​+q[h])=1/2 is constant. It describes drift in the translation direction to first order.

Each bx0−bb_{x_0}-bbx0​​−b belongs to H1H^1H1: write the difference as an integral of translated b′b'b′, and use the same argument with b′′b''b′′ for its derivative. On the affine class ϕ−b∈H1\phi-b\in H^1ϕ−b∈H1 and π=ϕt∈L2\pi=\phi_t\in L^2π=ϕt​∈L2, define distance to the static kink family by dorb(ϕ,π)=inf⁡x0∈R(∥ϕ−bx0∥H1+∥π∥2).d_{\mathrm{orb}}(\phi,\pi) =\inf_{x_0\in\R}\bigl(\|\phi-b_{x_0}\|_{H^1}+\|\pi\|_2\bigr).dorb​(ϕ,π)=x0​∈Rinf​(∥ϕ−bx0​​∥H1​+∥π∥2​). Orbital stability would require that for every ε>0\varepsilon>0ε>0 there is δ>0\delta>0δ>0 such that data with this distance below δ\deltaδ have distance below ε\varepsilonε for all future times. The factorisation does not prove this nonlinear assertion. It does not estimate the higher-order energy terms or choose a translating centre during nonlinear evolution. Theorem 16.3 supplies global energy-class evolution, but its continuation estimates allow growth and do not give this distance bound. Summary

The factorisation gives a nonnegative quadratic form and the exact translation kernel, with unit mode 3 sech⁡2x/2\sqrt3\,\sech^2x/23​sech2x/2.
