    The wave equation gives χtt(0)=Δp\chi_{tt}(0)=\Delta pχtt​(0)=Δp.     If all four potential data coincide, the first and third increments imply q=0q=0q=0 and ∇p=0\nabla p=0∇p=0.     Short answer

    Worked solution

    The compact source and the displayed perturbation conditions are exactly the hypotheses of Theorem 10.2. It supplies a smooth global perturbation (ϕ1,A1)(\phi_1,A_1)(ϕ1​,A1​) in Lorenz gauge.     Since ϕ1(t)∈H1\phi_1(t)\in H^1ϕ1​(t)∈H1, the scalar potential lies in ϕQ+H1\phi_Q+H^1ϕQ​+H1. If it also lay in H1H^1H1, subtraction of ϕ1\phi_1ϕ1​ would put ϕQ\phi_QϕQ​ in H1H^1H1, contradicting the preceding problem. This is a concrete construction around one fixed background, not a general existence theorem for arbitrary charged tails.
