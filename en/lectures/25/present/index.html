Lecture 25

Scattering data

We construct the Jost solutions by a convergent Volterra series. We prove the scattering flux identity with the course’s Wronskian order. Translation shows which information reflection probabilities and eigenvalues lose.

The operator of Lecture 24 replaces a field by a differential equation with a spectral parameter. At each nonzero real parameter, we construct solutions with prescribed behaviour at infinity. Their comparison gives complex scattering amplitudes. Bound states require additional data.

Summary

For real Schwartz potentials and real k≠0k\ne0k=0, the factorial Volterra bound constructs unique Jost solutions with both function and derivative tails. The constant Wronskian gives ∣a∣2−∣b∣2=1|a|^2-|b|^2=1∣a∣2−∣b∣2=1, so r=b/ar=b/ar=b/a is defined. The full right data also contain the right-normalised bound-state constants. Their inverse uniqueness is cited with its class and conventions. Translation preserves reflection probabilities and eigenvalues. Its formulas identify the phase and norming information lost by those reduced data. The elementary proof does not cover k=0k=0k=0.
