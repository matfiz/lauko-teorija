  • Exercise 3.3 Belinfante improvement from a spin identity #

    Hint

    Using antisymmetry in the last two slots of SSS gives Bμλν=12(−Sμνλ−Sλμν−Sνμλ)=−Bλμν.B^{\mu\lambda\nu} =\frac12\bigl(-S^{\mu\nu\lambda}-S^{\lambda\mu\nu} -S^{\nu\mu\lambda}\bigr)=-B^{\lambda\mu\nu}.Bμλν=21​(−Sμνλ−Sλμν−Sνμλ)=−Bλμν.
