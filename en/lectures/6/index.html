Lecture 6

The retarded kernel

What this lecture does

We prove the distributional identity for the retarded kernel, including its contribution at the cone vertex. We use the kernel to solve the wave equation for a smooth compactly supported source and determine where its field can occur.

Lecture 5 proves that a wave cannot propagate faster than unit speed. A source requires a further choice: its field must vanish before the source acts. We construct this solution for the wave operator in three spatial dimensions, with the convention □=∂t2−Δ\dAlembert=\partial_t^2-\Delta□=∂t2​−Δ.

6.1 The support condition

A distribution is a linear functional on test functions, continuous when their supports stay in a fixed compact set and all their derivatives converge uniformly. Its pairing ⟨T,ψ⟩\langle T,\psi\rangle⟨T,ψ⟩ is bilinear, without complex conjugation. Its support is the complement of the largest open set on whose tests it vanishes. 6.4 Retardation is an additional condition

If h∈C2(R1+3)h\in C^2(\R^{1+3})h∈C2(R1+3) solves □h=0\dAlembert h=0□h=0, then u+hu+hu+h still solves □(u+h)=f\dAlembert(u+h)=f□(u+h)=f. For example, h=1h=1h=1 gives a field which does not vanish before the source. The equation alone therefore does not select the retarded solution. Local uniqueness shows that a nonzero homogeneous addition cannot vanish for all sufficiently negative times. The advanced kernel Gadv=δ(t+∣x∣)/(4π∣x∣)\Gadv=\delta(t+|x|)/(4\pi|x|)Gadv​=δ(t+∣x∣)/(4π∣x∣) is also a fundamental solution, with support in the past cone.

The outgoing Helmholtz kernel of Analysis, Lecture 25, imposes a condition at spatial infinity for a fixed frequency. Retardation imposes a condition in time. Lectures 10 and 13 use the latter condition for fields produced by sources.

Summary

For □=∂t2−Δ\dAlembert=\partial_t^2-\Delta□=∂t2​−Δ on R1+3\R^{1+3}R1+3, the retarded kernel is defined by (6.1). Spherical averaging reduces its wave operator to an endpoint calculation. The vertex gives δ4\delta^4δ4 with coefficient one. Convolution with a smooth compact source gives the unique C2C^2C2 solution vanishing sufficiently early. The source is evaluated at the retarded time t−∣x−z∣t-|x-z|t−∣x−z∣. Adding a homogeneous solution preserves the equation but can violate the past condition.
