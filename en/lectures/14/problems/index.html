  • Exercise 14.4 A commuting Majorana-type real subspace #

    Return to Lecture 14’s two-component matrices α=−σ3\alpha=-\sigma_3α=−σ3​, β=σ1\beta=\sigma_1β=σ1​ and define the real-linear, antilinear-in-complex-scalars map Cψ=σ3ψ‾.\mathcal C\psi=\sigma_3\overline\psi.Cψ=σ3​ψ​. Substitution gives the real distributional equations at=ax+mb,bt=−bx−ma.a_t=a_x+mb,\qquad b_t=-b_x-ma.at​=ax​+mb,bt​=−bx​−ma. For two fixed tangent columns h=(a,ib)Th=(a,\ii b)^Th=(a,ib)T and g=(c,id)Tg=(c,\ii d)^Tg=(c,id)T, their inner product integrand is ac+bdac+bdac+bd, which is real.
